# Data sources and terms

This folder contains text from two published sources, output from four language models, and annotations made by the authors. The terms below are the authors' understanding; check the original sources before redistributing the data.

## Software engineering ethics (EQ2 reference, EQ3 software ethics set)

- **Source:** Software Engineering Code of Ethics and Professional Practice, version 5.2, by the ACM/IEEE-CS Joint Task Force on Software Engineering Ethics and Professional Practices.
- **Citation:** D. Gotterbarn, K. Miller, and S. Rogerson, "Software engineering code of ethics is approved," *Communications of the ACM*, vol. 42, no. 10, pp. 102–107, 1999.
- **Used here:** sentences from the Preamble and Principles 4, 7 and 8 (`eq3_software_ethics/prompt and fewshots/`, `eq3_software_ethics/curated_b1/`, and the EQ2 reference in `eq2_controlled_recovery/reference/`), quoted without changes.
- **Terms:** the Code is copyrighted by the ACM and the IEEE. Its published notice allows it to be reproduced without permission as long as it is not changed and carries the copyright notice: "Copyright © 1999 by the Association for Computing Machinery, Inc. and the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers, Inc." *(Please confirm the exact wording against the version of the Code you quote.)*

## Biomedical BEL (EQ3 biomedical set)

- **Source:** BioCreative V Track 4 corpus of Biological Expression Language (BEL) statements.
- **Citation:** J. Fluck, S. Madan, S. Ansari, A. T. Kodamullil, R. Karki, M. Rastegar-Mojarad, N. L. Catlett, W. Hayes, J. Szostak, J. Hoeng, and M. Peitsch, "Training and evaluation corpora for the extraction of causal relationships encoded in biological expression language (BEL)," *Database*, vol. 2016, baw113, 2016, doi:10.1093/database/baw113.
- **Used here:** 40 source sentences and their 69 reference BEL statements (`eq3_biomedical_bel/curated_b1/`) and 30 sentences used as demonstrations (`eq3_biomedical_bel/prompt and few shots/`).
- **Terms:** the corpus was released through the BioCreative challenge for research use, and the paper describing it is open access. *(Please confirm the licence of the corpus files themselves on the BioCreative or corpus download page before redistributing them.)*

## Model outputs (EQ3 candidates)

- `eq3_*/candidates/` holds the triples returned by ChatGPT-5.6-High and Gemini-3.1-Pro (web chat interfaces, 6 and 7 October 2026) and by GPT-OSS 120B and Qwen 3.8 27B (Groq API, temperature 0), all given the prompts in `prompt and few shots/` and `prompt and fewshots/`.
- `eq3_*/reproducibility_rerun/` holds the raw responses of a second run of the two Groq models.
- These outputs are included as research data. Use of each service is subject to its provider's terms.

## Annotations by the authors

- `eq3_*/curated_b1/`: the software ethics benchmark was curated by the two authors in TripleGrader. The biomedical benchmark is the corpus's own reference statements.
- `eq3_*/curation_sessions/`: the eight curation sessions exported from TripleGrader, with every grade, suggestion, approval and published version. The first author curated the biomedical sessions and the second author the software ethics sessions.
- `eq2_controlled_recovery/`: the injected errors, manifests and recovered exports are generated by the scripts in that folder from the reference above.
